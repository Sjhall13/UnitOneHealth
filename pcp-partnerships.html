Your patients, supported at home. The days after discharge are when your patients are most vulnerable — and when your office feels it most. Unit One Health covers the highest-risk window so you can focus on clinical care.

What We Handle

  • Day 1 in-home visit with vital signs and medication reconciliation
  • Telehealth check-ins on Days 3–5 and 9–10
  • Day 7 PCP visit coordination — we get them to you
  • SDOH barrier removal: transportation, medication access, food insecurity, home safety
  • Day 14 transition visit with a summary back to your office
  • Documentation designed to support CCM (99490), TCM (99495/99496), and RPM billing workflows

What This Means for Your Practice

  • Fewer after-hours calls from recently discharged patients
  • No more chasing no-shows for post-discharge follow-up
  • Timely visit summaries after every episode
  • Fewer avoidable ED visits and readmissions in your panel
