Core model: 14 days of high‑intensity stabilization for every patient, with optional 30‑day extended support for partners who need full readmission‑window coverage.

Phase 1: 14‑Day High‑Intensity Stabilization

  • Day 1 – Initial stabilization visit
  • Day 3–5 – Telehealth check‑in
  • Day 7 – PCP visit coordination
  • Day 9–10 – Telehealth check‑in
  • Day 14 – Final stabilization visit

Phase 2: Optional 30‑Day Extended Support

  • Day 18–20 – Telehealth check‑in
  • Day 24–26 – Medication + symptom review
  • Day 30 – Final transition summary

Why This Hybrid Model Matters

  • Clinically justified
  • Financially efficient
  • Flexible for partners
  • Provider‑friendly
