Welcome to Unit One Health

We provide high‑intensity stabilization during the most critical 14 days after discharge. Our team supports patients, families, and providers by reducing complications, addressing SDOH barriers, and coordinating early medical escalations with the PCP.

Leadership

Scott Hall, BSN, MSN-Ed, RN
Founder, Chief Executive Officer & Compliance Officer

A nurse and veteran, Scott founded Unit One Health to close the most dangerous gap in healthcare: the days after discharge. Unit One Health is a veteran-owned, nurse-led organization built on evidence-based transitional care.

“To reduce preventable hospital readmissions and improve post-discharge health outcomes through evidence-based, nurse-led transitional care delivered with compassion, clinical excellence, and unwavering integrity.”
